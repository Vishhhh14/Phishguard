ABOUT PHISHGUARD

Understanding the Threat.

PhishGuard is a web-based phishing detection system designed to help users identify potentially suspicious emails and messages.

Explainable

PhishGuard shows the indicators that contributed to a warning instead of providing only a final verdict.

Rule-Based

The detection engine analyzes predefined phishing indicators and combinations of suspicious patterns.

Risk Analysis

Messages are assigned a risk score and classified as Safe, Suspicious, or Phishing.

HOW IT WORKS

From Message to Result.

PhishGuard follows a simple analysis process to identify common phishing indicators.

01

Paste

Paste a suspicious email or message into the analysis box.

02

Analyze

The system checks the message for multiple phishing indicators.

03

Score

Detected indicators contribute to the overall risk score.

04

PhishGuard displays the risk level and explains the detected indicators.
